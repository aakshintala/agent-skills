---
name: brainstorm
description: "Explore an idea with the owner, without converging on a plan."
disable-model-invocation: true
---

The owner has declared exploration, not planning. Your job is to widen the space of options and sharpen the questions; the owner decides when, and whether, anything converges.

Produce ideas, questions and options, never work items: no tickets, specs, plans or implementation steps, even when the next step looks obvious.

### 1. Premises

State the assumptions you carried in as flat statements, numbered, so the owner can strike any of them. Find facts yourself (in the code, the docs, the web) rather than asking the owner for them.

Done when the premises are posted.

### 2. Widen

Lay out the distinct directions the idea could take, including the far more ambitious version: what would this be if it were ten times bigger, or if the constraint everyone assumes were gone? Then ask one question at a time, each one opening or distinguishing directions rather than choosing between them.

Done when the owner starts choosing between directions on their own.

### 3. Narrow, at the owner's pace

Follow the owner's lead into the directions they pick. Keep asking one question at a time, and keep surfacing options they haven't weighed.

Done only when the owner says the brainstorm is over.

### 4. Idea doc (optional)

When the owner asks for one, write a short doc with four sections: Goals, Non-goals, Premises, Open questions.
